The one rule

Money is added only when two numbers are exactly the same

The screenshot is only what the customer says. The bank's SMS or statement is the proof. The example plays the four cases by itself. Tap any case to stop on it.

Screenshot = the customer's claim Bank SMS or statement = the proof UTR + amount must match exactly
Who is involved

Four parts work together

Customer

Pays and uploads

Chooses an amount, pays from any UPI app or bank, uploads the success screenshot. Never types a UTR.

Our server

Reads and matches

Reads the screenshot, runs the checks, and compares it with the bank's records. Adds money only on an exact match.

Bank side

Reports real money

Each HDFC account has a phone with our Android app. It forwards HDFC's credit SMS. A bank statement is also uploaded a few times a day.

Admin

Decides the rest

When the system cannot be sure, a person looks up the UTR in the bank and approves or rejects. Also uploads statements.

Phone on, internet on

The SMS is read and sent to the server in a few seconds. This is the normal case.

Result: credited in seconds

Phone on, no internet

An SMS does not need internet, so it still arrives. The app keeps it in a queue and sends it by itself when the internet is back, even if the phone restarted meanwhile.

Result: credited when back online

Phone switched off, or no signal

The SMS cannot reach the phone. The mobile network normally holds it and delivers it when the phone is on again; then the app forwards it.

Result: credited after the phone is back

SMS never comes, phone lost or broken

Nothing is guessed. The customer sees "Bank confirmation is late". The next bank statement upload credits the payment. A new phone gets a new code; the old code stops working.

Result: credited from the statement

In all four cases the dashboard shows the phone as Offline after 40 minutes without contact and reminds the admin. No payment is lost, because the bank statement always has every UTR. The Android app builds, but it has not been run on a real phone yet.

Admin screens

What the team sees and does

The dashboard uses plain words. Most payments never need a person; these screens are for the ones that do, and for the daily bank statement.

Daily routine

  1. Clear "Payments to check". Target: within 4 hours.
  2. Upload each account's bank statement at 10 AM, 2 PM, 6 PM and 10 PM, with the time it was downloaded.
  3. Act on every alert on the Overview page, then press Done.
  4. Check every phone is online under Bank accounts.

How a person verifies a payment

  1. Open "Payments to check" and press Check on the card.
  2. Copy the UTR shown and search it in that HDFC account's statement.
  3. Found: press "Found in bank". Use the UTR and amount exactly as the bank shows them.
  4. Not found: check the other accounts too (customers sometimes pay an old saved QR), then press "Not in bank".

Reasons a customer can see after a rejection: no payment with this UTR reached our account · the payment went to an account that isn't ours · this UTR belongs to another customer's payment · this screenshot doesn't match any payment we received.

Run the real app on your own computer

The real app is a small server (it reads screenshots and keeps a database), so it cannot run on this page. Needs Python 3.9 or newer. Run this inside the project folder, then open http://localhost:8000/wallet and http://localhost:8000/admin (password demo123).

cd server
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
ADMIN_TOKEN=demo123 DEMO_MODE=1 .venv/bin/uvicorn main:app --port 8000
